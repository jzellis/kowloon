# Code of Conduct

Kowloon is a small, human-scale project, and we want it to feel that way —
welcoming, honest, and free of the ugliness the big platforms taught us to
expect. This applies to everyone: maintainers, contributors, testers, and anyone
hanging around our repos, Discussions, or the servers we run
([kwln.social](https://kwln.social), [kwln.city](https://kwln.city)).

## The short version

Be decent. Assume good faith. Disagree without being cruel. We're all here
because we want something better than what we've got.

## Expected of everyone

- Treat people with respect and patience — especially newcomers and testers.
- Give (and take) feedback in good faith. Critique the work, not the person.
- Respect that people arrive with different backgrounds, skill levels, and ways
  of communicating.
- Keep discussion relevant and constructive.

## Not welcome here

- Harassment, insults, or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning remarks based on who someone is — race, gender,
  sexuality, disability, religion, nationality, age, appearance, or the like.
- Sexual language or imagery, or unwanted sexual attention.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their consent.
- Sustained disruption, trolling, or bad-faith argument.
- Spam or self-promotion unrelated to Kowloon.

## Scope

This applies anywhere the project lives: the GitHub organization (repositories,
issues, Discussions, pull requests) and the community on kwln.social and
kwln.city.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior is making this space worse, tell us. Reach the maintainer
privately by DMing **@jzellis@kwln.social**. Reports are handled discreetly, and
you won't be penalized for reporting in good faith.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove content, lock threads, and warn, mute, or ban
anyone who breaks these rules — with a response proportional to what happened.
Josh Ellis, as the project maintainer, has the final say.
